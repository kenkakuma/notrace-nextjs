Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026

OLLA Brew Lab (“OLLA”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) is a local-first specialty coffee logging and reference tool. Our core principle is simple: your personal coffee data — such as brew logs, bean cards, gear records, favorites, notes, and preferences — is stored on your device and is not sold.

1. Information We Collect

The current release does not require an account, sign-up, or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or advertising identifier.

Content you create in the app — including brew logs, bean cards or jars, gear records, favorites, notes, and preferences — is stored locally on your device, inside the iOS app sandbox. We do not upload this content to our servers or sell it to third parties.

We do not use App Tracking Transparency, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

2. System Permissions

The app does not request access to camera, photos, precise location, contacts, or microphone.

If you enable local bean reminders, the app will request the iOS notification permission. These reminders are scheduled on your device according to your settings and can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings or inside the app where available.

The app may use on-device haptic feedback. Haptic feedback does not involve data collection.

3. Network Use

The app is designed to work locally, and core features can be used without an account.

The app may connect to the network for limited purposes:

  • Dictionary updates: the app may make anonymous requests to our content service to check for and download public coffee reference content. These requests may include a “last updated” cursor so the app can fetch only new or changed content.
  • Industry reference content: the app may fetch public competition, auction, or industry reference content from the same service.
  • Connectivity check: the app may perform a no-content reachability check to determine whether network access is available.

These requests do not include your bean cards, brew logs, notes, favorites, or other personal coffee data. We do not include a user account ID or advertising identifier.

Standard server logs may temporarily process technical request information such as IP address, timestamp, request path, and user agent for security, debugging, and reliability. We do not use this information to identify users, build user profiles, or track users across apps or websites.

Fonts are bundled with the app and are not downloaded at runtime.

4. Your Control Over Your Data

You control the data you create in the app.

In Settings → Data, you can export your data as a full JSON backup, export brew records as CSV where available, import or restore a backup, or erase all local app data.

Uninstalling the app deletes the local app data stored on your device, subject to normal iOS behavior and any device backups you control.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or server-side user profiles for your app content, most data access, export, correction, and deletion actions can be performed directly through the app’s local data tools. You may also contact us for privacy questions or requests.

5. Security and Retention

Your app content is stored in the iOS application sandbox and protected by iOS system security and device-level protections. Backups exported from the app are files under your control; you are responsible for storing and sharing them safely.

Local app data is retained until you delete it, erase app data, or uninstall the app, subject to any backups you choose to create.

6. Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store and may use Apple system services for app installation, updates, notifications, and device-level functionality. Apple’s handling of data is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

Our content service provides public coffee reference content and does not receive your bean cards, brew logs, notes, favorites, or other personal coffee data.

The current release does not integrate advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, social-login SDKs, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. If such services are added in a future release, this policy and our App Store privacy information will be updated accordingly.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience of coffee enthusiasts and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Users

The app may be available in multiple countries and regions. If you are located in the EEA, UK, California, Japan, or another region with privacy rights, you may contact us about privacy-related questions or requests.

Because the app does not require user accounts and stores your personal coffee content locally, you can manage most of your app data directly through the export, import, and delete features in the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide notice in the app or on our website.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: kokennka@gmail.com
